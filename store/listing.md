## English

**Subtitle (App Store, 30):** How many holes does it have?

**Short description (Google Play, 80):** A mug is a donut. Guess the topology of everyday objects and dare your friends.

**Keywords (App Store, 100):** topology,donut,puzzle,math,holes,genus,torus,brain,quiz,daily,geometry,trivia,logic

**Promotional text (App Store, 170):** To a topologist, a coffee mug and a donut are the same thing. Can you see the world that way? One new object every day.

**Description:**

To a topologist, a coffee mug and a donut are the same thing: each has exactly one hole. A T-shirt? Three. A drinking straw? One, and people will argue with you about it.

Mugnut shows you an everyday object and asks one question: what is it, topologically? Count the holes that go all the way through, pick sphere, torus, double torus or beyond, and find out whether your intuition survives.

• 257 hand-drawn objects, from spoons and scissors to pretzels and dungarees
• After each answer, see exactly where the holes are
• A new object every day, the same for everyone, with a streak to keep
• Journey mode: climb from Easy to Expert on three lives
• Themed packs: Kitchen, Clothing, Tools, Letters & digits (Latin and Armenian), and Controversies, the ones people fight about
• Photograph anything around you and challenge a friend to guess its topology
• English, Armenian and Russian

Free to play: Easy and Medium levels, the daily object and friend challenges. One small purchase unlocks Hard, Expert and every pack, for good. No ads, no subscription, no account, and the app collects no data.

**What's new (1.0):** The first release.
